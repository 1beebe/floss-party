Floss Party privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Floss Party is a daily floss tracker. It is built so that your data never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Floss Party has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The days you mark as flossed, plus your sound and theme settings, are stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage, or inside the app on iPhone). We cannot see them, and they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What leaves your device

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device asks Google's servers for the font files, the same way any website that uses Google Fonts does. No floss data is included in that request.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data, or deleting the app, erases your history completely.

Children

Floss Party collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds sign-in or syncing, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the new features will be optional.
